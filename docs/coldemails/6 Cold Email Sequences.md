# 6 Cold Email Sequences — CMH2.tech

Two sequences per niche. Each sequence has 2 emails: the initial pitch + one casual follow-up.

**Variables used (set up in Instantly):**
- `{{firstName}}` — prospect's first name
- `{{companyName}}` — prospect's company name
- `{{icebreaker}}` — AI-generated personalization line (via Gemini in Sheets)

**Schedule:** Monday–Saturday, 7:00am–7:00pm ET
**Follow-up delay:** 3 days after Email 1

---

## Alternative CTA: Loom Video Strategy

Based on Sav's $500K cold email framework (via Nate Herk): instead of asking the prospect to reply "yes" or book a call, offer to send a short Loom video. This is lower friction — they don't commit to anything, just watch a video.

**How to use:** Replace the CTA in any sequence above with one of these:

For Free Build offers:
```
Would it be worth sending you a 90-second video showing how it works? No commitment — just a quick walkthrough.
```

For Free Audit offers:
```
I put together a 2-minute video walking through how this works for firms like {{companyName}}. Want me to send it over?
```

**Additional subject line variations to A/B test:**
- `Q: {{companyName}} taking on more clients?`
- `Q from your neighbor {{firstName}}`
- `{{firstName}}, thought of {{companyName}}`

**Niche directory strategy:** Before scraping Apollo, search for industry-specific databases (e.g., AIA for architects, AICPA for accountants, industry association member lists). These give higher-quality, pre-filtered leads. Use Apollo only to enrich with email addresses.

## Setup Notes for Instantly

1. **Create 6 campaigns** in Instantly (one per sequence)
2. **Strip all markdown formatting** when pasting — these should be plain text emails
3. **Set follow-up delay** to 3 days
4. **Schedule:** Monday–Saturday, 7:00am–7:00pm ET
5. **Make sure `{{icebreaker}}`** is mapped to your custom variable column in your lead CSV
6. **Start with 25 emails/day per mailbox** — don't ramp up until you've confirmed reply rates are healthy (>1%)
7. After ~300 sends per sequence, compare A vs B within each niche and kill the loser
8. Once you find a winner with >2% reply rate, you can add a 3rd follow-up email to the sequence
