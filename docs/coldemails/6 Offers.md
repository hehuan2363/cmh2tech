# 6 Cold Email Offers — CMH2.tech

Three niches, two offers each. Use these as the core offer statement in your cold email campaigns. A/B test both offers per niche to find the winner.

---

## Niche 1: Property Management Companies

**Why this niche:** You run your own rental portfolio and built Rivvet.ca (AI invoice tracker for landlords). You understand their workflows firsthand — vendor invoices, maintenance requests, tenant communication, bookkeeping. You're not a consultant guessing at their problems; you live them.

**Apollo search filters:**
- Job titles: Property Manager, Operations Manager, Director of Operations, Owner
- Company size: 10-200 employees
- Industry: Real Estate, Property Management
- Location: US & Canada

### Offer 1A — Invoice Automation Bot (Free Build)

> I'll build you a custom bot that reads your vendor invoices, pulls out the key data (amount, vendor, category, date), and logs it straight to your spreadsheet or accounting system — completely free. I'll do all the work myself and have it running within 72 hours. Just reply "yes" and send me one sample invoice.

**Why it works:**
- Minimizes financial risk: It's free.
- Minimizes friction: They just reply "yes" and attach one invoice. Dead simple.
- Cheap to produce: You already built this with Rivvet.ca. Repurposing your existing system for a new client takes minimal effort.
- Very valuable to them: Every PM company wastes hours on manual invoice entry.

### Offer 1B — 3 Workflow Automation Audit (Free)

> I manage my own rental properties and automated everything — invoicing, tenant comms, maintenance tracking. I'll do the same audit for your operation: identify your top 3 time-wasting workflows and show you exactly how to automate each one. No cost, takes 15 minutes of your time. Just reply "interested" and I'll send a booking link.

**Why it works:**
- Minimizes financial risk: Free.
- Minimizes friction: 15 minutes on a call, they reply with one word.
- Cheap to produce: A 15-minute discovery call that leads to a templated audit doc you can reuse across PM clients.
- Credible: You open with the fact that you run your own properties. That's peer-to-peer, not consultant-to-client.

You've solved this exact problem at enterprise scale.

- Company size: 50-500 employees
- Industry: Engineering Services, Architecture, Environmental Services, Consulting
- Location: US & Canada

## Testing Strategy

- Run both offers per niche simultaneously (A/B test)
- Send ~200-300 emails per offer before deciding a winner
- Track **reply rate** (not open rate). Target: >3% reply rate
- The "Free Build" offers (A) will likely get higher-quality replies but lower volume
- The "Free Audit" offers (B) will likely get more replies but need stronger follow-up to convert
- Kill any offer below 1% reply rate after 300 sends and write a new variation
