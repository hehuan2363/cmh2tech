# Revised Marketing Strategy & Action Plan for CMH2.tech

**Prepared for:** Huan He, Founder of CMH2.tech
**Date:** February 9, 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines a revised marketing strategy for CMH2.tech, specifically adapted to address the valid concerns of audience overlap on LinkedIn and the confidentiality of your enterprise-level projects. The core objective remains to establish CMH2.tech as a leading provider of AI and automation services for Small to Medium-sized Businesses (SMBs). However, the approach is now more nuanced, prioritizing discretion and a gradual brand build.

The cornerstone of this revised strategy is the **"Gradual Pivot"** on LinkedIn. Instead of an immediate, aggressive launch, we will first establish your profile as a general thought leader in AI and automation within regulated and professional environments. This builds your authority without creating conflict with your current role. We will simultaneously build your brand on channels you fully control, such as a dedicated CMH2.tech website.

Your most powerful, publicly-shareable assets—the **Rivvet.ca invoice tracker** and the **automation of your personal real estate business**—will be elevated as the primary case studies. Your impressive enterprise experience, including the $1.4M in productivity gains, will be used as a powerful credibility statement, framed in an aggregate, confidential manner. This strategy mitigates risk while still leveraging your most compelling strengths to attract high-value SMB clients.

## 4. Core Messaging & Positioning (Revised)

We will adjust the messaging to lead with your shareable experience and frame your enterprise results more discreetly.

**Unique Value Proposition (UVP):**

> **CMH2.tech delivers practical, high-ROI automation for small businesses, built with the precision of a nuclear engineer and the pragmatism of a fellow entrepreneur. We turn complex AI capabilities into reliable systems that save you time and money.**

### Key Messaging Pillars (Revised):

1.  **From Your Side of the Table (Lead with this):** "I don't just consult on automation; I use it every day to run my own successful rental business. I understand your challenges because I've solved them for myself with tools like Rivvet.ca, an invoice tracker I built from the ground up."

2.  **Engineer's Precision, Entrepreneur's Pragmatism:** "As a nuclear engineer, I build systems that are reliable and secure. As a business owner, I build systems that are practical and profitable."

3.  **Proven at Scale (The Credibility Anchor):** "In my day job, I've applied these same principles to generate over $1.4 million in productivity gains for a large engineering firm. While the details are confidential, the strategic thinking is not. I bring that enterprise-level experience to every SMB project."

This revised messaging structure allows you to lead with your most open and relatable stories while using your impressive enterprise experience as a powerful, but confidential, proof point.
## 5. Phased Marketing & Sales Action Plan (Revised: The "Gradual Pivot")

This revised plan is designed for discretion and sustainable growth, allowing you to build your brand without creating conflict with your current employment.

### Phase 1: Foundation & Thought Leadership (Months 1-3) - "Stealth Mode"

The goal is to build your authority and assets behind the scenes, without overt promotion of CMH2.tech on LinkedIn.

| **2-4** | **Develop Core Case Studies (for Website)** | - Write detailed case studies for your **publicly shareable** projects:<br>  1. **The Automated Real Estate Business:** (CMH2 Properties)<br>  2. **The AI Invoice Processor:** (Rivvet.ca)<br>  3. **The Open-Source Article Writer**<br>- Frame your enterprise experience as an aggregate success story on your "About" page. |

| **6-12** | **Begin Thought Leadership on LinkedIn** | - Post 2-3 times per week about **general AI and automation topics**.<br>- **Content Themes:** AI in regulated industries, balancing innovation and safety, practical automation tips for professionals.<br>- **Goal:** Become known as an AI expert, not a freelancer looking for work. |

## 6. Channel Strategy: Diversification is Key

Given your constraints, a multi-channel approach is crucial. LinkedIn is a tool, but not your entire strategy.

1.  **Your Website (Primary Hub):** This is where your brand lives. All marketing efforts should ultimately drive traffic here. You have 100% control over the content and messaging.

2.  **LinkedIn (The "Slow Burn"):** Use the "Gradual Pivot" strategy. It's a long-term play to build authority and network. It will become more powerful over time as your audience shifts.

3.  **Niche Communities (Targeted Outreach):** Engage in forums like BiggerPockets for real estate investors or industry-specific groups where your target clients gather. This provides direct access with no audience overlap.

4.  **Referral Partnerships (High-Trust Leads):** Collaborate with accountants, bookkeepers, and IT consultants who already serve your ICPs. A referral from a trusted advisor is the warmest lead you can get.

## 9. Final Recommendations

Your concerns are valid and wise. A "blitz" marketing campaign on LinkedIn is not the right move. A more sophisticated, patient, and diversified approach will serve you better in the long run.

1.  **Embrace the "Gradual Pivot":** It protects your current career while building your future business.
2.  **Lead with Your Public Wins:** Your rental business and Rivvet.ca are powerful, relatable case studies for the SMB market. Make them the heroes of your story.
3.  **Use Enterprise Experience as a Credibility Anchor:** Frame the "$1.4M" as proof of your capabilities, not as a detailed case study. The line, "While the details are confidential, the strategic thinking is not," is key.
4.  **Build Your Own Platform:** Your website is your castle. Invest your primary energy there, where you control the narrative completely.

This revised strategy allows you to start immediately, build momentum safely, and position CMH2.tech for long-term success without jeopardizing your current professional standing.
